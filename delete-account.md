# Delete your Savia account


## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Savia.
2. Go to **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and hold the button to confirm.

Your garden is hidden straight away.

## Changed your mind?

We keep your account for **30 days**. Sign back in within 30 days, with the same sign-in you used before, and everything comes back just as you left it. A guest garden (you never signed in) cannot be restored and is removed straight away.

## After 30 days

Your account is deleted for good:
- Your sign-in details, including your email.
- Your plants, spaces, notes, places, care records, reminders and settings.
- Your link to Apple or Google sign-in, and your phone's reminder registration.

## What we keep

- **Photos, without you.** Plant photos you sent stay, with every link to you removed: account, device, place, notes and photo details. We use them to test and improve how Savia names plants and checks problems. Nothing in them points back to you.
- **Feedback** you sent us, anonymous.
- **Purchase and tax records** that the law says we must keep.
- **Backups.** Deleted data can sit in our backups for up to ten weeks after the 30 days, then it is gone.

## Before you delete

- After 30 days it cannot be undone. Use Export in Account first if you want a copy of your garden.
- **Deleting does not cancel Premium.** Cancel in the App Store or Google Play first, or you may still be charged. Steps are on our [Support page](https://quos-ai.github.io/savia-pages/support).
- Free plant identification keeps working without an account.

## Can't open the app?

You can ask us to delete your account without the app. Email **support@savia.quos.ai** with the subject "Delete my Savia account".

- Write from the email you signed in with. If you signed in with Apple or Google, tell us which one.
- Never send a password, a sign-in code or a private photo. We will never ask for one.
- We check the request comes from you, then reply within one month. Deletion follows the same steps as above: hidden straight away, kept 30 days, then removed.
- If you never signed in (a guest garden) and lost the phone, we cannot match you to a garden. Tell us what you can and we will do what we can.
