# Delete your Savia account


## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Savia.
2. Go to **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and hold the button to confirm.

Your garden is hidden straight away.

## Changed your mind?

We keep your account for **30 days**. Sign back in within 30 days, with the same sign-in you used before, and everything comes back just as you left it. If you never signed in (a guest garden), it can only come back on the same phone.

## After 30 days

Your account is deleted for good:
- Your sign-in details, including your email.
- Your plants, spaces, notes, places, care records, reminders and settings.
- Your link to Apple or Google sign-in.

## What we keep

- **Photos, without you.** Plant photos you sent stay, with every link to you removed: account, device, place, notes and photo details. We use them to test and improve how Savia names plants and checks problems. Nothing in them points back to you.
- **Feedback** you sent us, anonymous.
- **Purchase and tax records** that the law says we must keep.
- **Backups.** Deleted data can sit in our backups for up to ten weeks after the 30 days, then it is gone.

## Before you delete

- After 30 days it cannot be undone. Use Export in Account first if you want a copy of your garden.
- **Deleting does not cancel Premium.** Cancel in the App Store or Google Play first, or you may still be charged. Steps are on our [Support page](support)(https://savia.quos.ai/support).
- Free plant identification keeps working without an account.
