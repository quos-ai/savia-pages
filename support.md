# Savia Support


We are happy to help with your plants and your app.

## Contact us

Email support@savia.quos.ai. We usually reply within two working days. 

Please tell us:
- what happened, and what you expected;
- your phone model and system version;
- your Savia version (Account, then the bottom of the page);
- a screenshot, if it helps. Please leave out photos of other people.

## Quick answers

**My plant name looks wrong.** Savia gives its best guess and says when it is unsure. Try a clearer photo in daylight with the leaf or flower in the middle. You can change the name any time.

**My plant looks sick. Can I trust the answer?** Treat it as a helpful starting point, not a diagnosis. If a plant is valuable or the problem is spreading, ask a local nursery.

**Is this plant safe for my pet or child?** Savia shows what it knows. It can be incomplete. If a pet or person may have eaten a plant, call your vet, doctor or local poison control straight away.

**I can't get my garden back after changing phones.** Sign in with the same email, Apple or Google account you used before. If you never signed in, the garden stays on the phone it was made on, so sign in before you switch.

**My reminders did not come.** Check that notifications are on for Savia in your phone settings, and that battery saver is not blocking them.

**How do I cancel Premium?** Through the store you bought it from. See [Refunds and cancelling](refunds).
- iPhone: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Savia.
- Android: Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Savia.
Deleting your Savia account does not cancel it.

**Can I get a refund?** Apple and Google handle refunds. See [Refunds and cancelling](refunds).

**How do I restore Premium on a new phone?** Open Account, Subscription, then Restore.

**How do I turn usage data off?** Account, Privacy Policy, then the on/off switch at the bottom.

**How do I delete my account?** See [Delete account](https://quos-ai.github.io/savia-pages/delete-account).
